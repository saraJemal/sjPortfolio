About Me

I am a software developer and IT solutions provider with BSc degrees in Electrical Engineering from Hawassa University and Information Technology from International Open University. I specialize in developing practical, user-friendly web applications and digital solutions that help schools, businesses, and organizations improve efficiency, simplify their operations, and achieve their goals. My portfolio includes a School Information Management System, a school website, and an Online Exam Web Service.

These projects demonstrate my ability to transform practical needs into functional digital solutions. Beyond development, I provide website maintenance, technical support, and software customization to help clients keep their digital solutions running smoothly and adapt them as their needs evolve. My goal is to build reliable, tailored technology solutions and establish lasting relationships with clients through quality work, responsive support, and continuous improvement. Web Application

Online Exam Web Service (OEWS)

A web-based recruitment assessment solution designed to help organizations manage examinations for shortlisted applicants, from exam scheduling and question preparation to timed assessments and result reporting.

Key Features

  • Administrator account and user management.
  • HR exam scheduling and question management.
  • Importing exam questions from Excel.
  • Timed examinations with automatic submission.
  • Automatic score calculation and result reports.
School Information System

School Management System

A role-based school information management system that brings important school activities together for administrators, teachers, students, and parents.

Key Features

  • Student enrollment and attendance management.
  • Assessment results, report cards, and performance analysis.
  • Teacher and student dashboards.
  • Class schedules, announcements, and school events.
  • Communication Book for parent–teacher communication.
  • Parent access to their children's results and attendance.
